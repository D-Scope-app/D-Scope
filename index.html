FAQ

Questions privacy-first teams ask first.

D-Scope is completing final validation for its public testnet release. The working product validates verified eligibility, unique private participation, finalization, and aggregate result flows.

How is D-Scope different from a simple verified poll?

D-Scope is designed for research campaigns where audience policy, unique participation, privacy thresholds, finalization, and segmented aggregate insight matter together.

Does D-Scope store passport documents?

D-Scope is designed not to store raw identity documents such as passport images, document numbers, MRZ data, or names in regular product storage. It works with minimized eligibility outputs needed for participation and aggregate analytics.

Can campaign creators see individual respondent profiles?

The creator-facing model is aggregate-first. Campaign creators receive finalized results and privacy-checked segment analytics rather than exposed respondent-level identity records.

What happens when a segment is too small?

Small segments can remain suppressed or be grouped into broader buckets until the configured privacy threshold is met.

Will D-Scope support more eligibility signals and campaign modules?

Yes. D-Scope is designed as a modular system so additional eligibility sources, research dimensions, and campaign modules can be introduced without changing the aggregate-first creator experience.

Where can I review the documentation and code?

The current codebase, architecture overview, security policy, and known testnet limitations are available in the public D-Scope GitHub repository.
